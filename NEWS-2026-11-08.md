### 7.1 `2611.07605` — "The Gating Mechanism of KV Cache Eviction" (submitted 2026-11-05)

Eviction as a **learnable gate inside the softmax**: "That same normalizer absorbs any per-query constant, so **a
constant gate cancels and reproduces the frozen model exactly**." Trained on the language-modelling loss the gate "stays
near a constant score while its loss falls throughout: optimization succeeds, but a frozen backbone was fit for dense
attention, so **its loss prefers the no-op**".
**Why it is on our axis:** our KV-sized decisions (`--kv int8`, `k8v4`, `--kv-resident`) are all "keep fewer bytes"
claims, and this says the model's own objective cannot price the loss of a cache entry - so an eviction/compression
knob that looks free under a perplexity instrument is measuring the instrument, not the model. It is the KV analogue of
`2610.37126`'s pruning result that our 2026-10-29 entry adopted (perplexity must be paired with a task-level check).

### 7.2 `2611.07217` — "TileQuant: Tile as the Precision-Allocation Unit for LLM Mixed-Precision PTQ" (2026-11-04)

Training-free mixed-precision PTQ that assigns precision to **hardware-aligned 2D weight tiles** rather than to whole
columns, because "sensitivity varies within columns, so whole-column retention also protects less sensitive weights,
while runtime outlier detection adds decoding overhead".
**Why it is on our axis:** it names the axis a pack publisher actually chooses. Every quant set we hold (`q2_0`,
`IQ1_M`, `UD-IQ4_XS`) is a **per-tensor** decision made by someone else; the next lever is *within* the tensor, and this
is the training-free form of it. No action - the pack is the vendor's call - but it gives a name to the gap between
"the same quant name" and "the same bytes".

### 7.3 `2611.07654` — "English Calibration Erodes Lithuanian in Expert-Pruned Mixture-of-Experts Language Models" (2026-11-05)

REAP pruning of four MoE models, removing **25 % or 50 %** of experts under **English, Lithuanian or mixed**
calibration, then reading comprehension, translation and language modelling in both languages:
**at 25 % pruning, English comprehension fell a median of 1.7 points but Lithuanian fell 21.4.** The transfer is
asymmetric - Lithuanian calibration "removed most of the Lithuanian loss and never significantly hurt English".
**Why it is on our axis, and this is the strongest item in the section:** we run a **learned expert map**
(`--expert-profile`, 24,576 ranked pairs in a 196,632-byte `.strp`, `NEWS` 2026-10-16) and we have never tested its
provenance. If a 25 % expert decision tuned on one distribution costs 21 points on another, then **the profile file is a
task-specific instrument, not a neutral optimisation** - and our copy was learned from whatever prompts produced it.
**Instrument:** the 414-check suite, run with and without `--expert-profile`, on two task shapes. **Cost:** two starts,
no download.
