| Arm | reused | re-read | Append TTFT, 5 reps (s) | Decode t/s |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| tail switch **off** (production) | 123,648 x5 | **11,797 x5** | 6.72 / 6.86 / 6.74 / 6.81 / 6.73 | 95.1 |
| tail switch **on** | 123,648 x5 | **11,797 x5** | 8.77 / 7.66 / 7.08 / 7.09 / 6.96 | 95.4 |

**(b) `STRATA_PLE_AUTO_RAM=0` buys a bigger chunk and pays for it three ways** - `jesse890423`,
**2026-11-17T20:58:01Z**. RTX 5090 32 GB, 128 GiB RAM, engine 0.1.60 / Terminal 0.2.15, UD-Q4_K_XL, 400K YaRN

- **arm A (default)**: `prompt chunk auto: 8192 tokens`, `resident RAM mode: 55.28 GiB of experts in RAM

- **arm B (`STRATA_PLE_AUTO_RAM=0`)**: `prompt chunk auto: 32768 tokens`, `cache complement ready: resident

  working-set minimum + VirtualLock`, no table lock, `PLE direct read probe: 284486 rows/s`.

His conclusion is the item: **"per-turn speed, KV-reuse depth and CPU all get worse, and none of the three is a
quality trade"** - so the chunk-8192 -> 32768 gain is bought with a lost page-locked tier and the
working-set/VirtualLock fallback, which is the same
`page-locking refused (out of memory); trying pageable memory` line this lab recorded on 2026-11-01 and
re-read in `#6074` on 11-16. **A fix whose own log shows the platform limit it hit is the clearest form of
"declare the fallback".**

**Net for the declarations:** append cost stays a first-class metric, `--prompt-cache-tail` is struck from the
candidate list for the 16 GB/17,664 shape, and `STRATA_PLE_AUTO_RAM` joins `STRATA_ADAPT_FETCH` and
`STRATA_WINDOW_WARM` as a switch that must be declared because its default changed behaviour under us.

## Shortlist, cheapest first

1. **Fold the first-request-after-start rule into the protocol, because two independent reports this window put
   the artifact there** (`#6171`, `#6181` section 1). With `STRATA_ROUTE_PRIOR` on, engine 0.2.15 gives P(`<|im_end|>`)
   a median of **4.8-7.4 %** on the first request against **1.0 %** on 0.2.13.1, and **10 of 137 answers came out
   short against 0 of 48**; `STRATA_WINDOW_WARM=0` takes it to 1.7 %, prior-off to 1.4 %. Separately, a 12 GB
   community benchmark saw the first request after a load be **the slowest in every arm** and could not say why.
   So: **discard the first request of every arm from the scored set, report it separately, and declare
   `STRATA_WINDOW_WARM`**. This changes how rows we already hold are read. (An edit to the declaration template.)
2. **Strike `--prompt-cache-tail` from the append-cost candidates and keep the two switches that work**
   (section 4). On a 16 GB card / `prefill_cap` 17,664 the tail switch is **live and inert** - `reused`
   123,648 and re-read **11,797** identical across ten reps, with the flag asserted from the engine's own argv -
   and `STRATA_PLE_AUTO_RAM=0` buys chunk 8192 -> 32768 while its own log shows `pinned 0.00 GiB`,
   `page-locking refused (out of memory)`, and a working-set/VirtualLock fallback. What survives from the 11-17
   item is `STRATA_PREFILL_EQUAL=0` (+`CPU_SHARE=0`) and a real append metric. (An edit.)
3. **Read the display-to-iGPU change as a two-condition experiment, not a cable** (section 2, and `#5874` from
   11-13). Same card, same config, same day: desktop on the RTX 5070 reads **33-41 / 42-48 tok/s** (short / 30K)
   and **~1,170** prompt at 30K; desktop on the Radeon 780M reads **46-49 / 57** and **~1,370**. Meanwhile
   *enabling* the iGPU with the display still on the card makes the VRAM probe read 0 GiB and no start possible.
   So the arms are **display on card** and **display moved**, with the probe line read back, and a start failure
   is the documented outcome of the half-measure. (Kevin's cable; the software half is one start each.)
4. **Make `page-locking refused (out of memory)` a declaration field with a VOID condition attached** - it has
   now appeared inside a *fix* (section 4b), in basalt's stall report (`0 MiB RAM available`, section 10), and
   in our own 2026-11-01 note. A start that logs it is measuring pageable memory, and a start whose page-locked
   RAM does not come back after a kill (44.6 GiB, section 10) is a reboot. (An edit; the check is one grep.)
5. **Decide the `#6111` build question before the bump**: `STRATA_OWN_CPU_QUANT` is **ON by default on x86_64**
   in 0.2.15 and the microbenchmark is 4 % slower on the K-quants and **20-22 % slower on `q8_0`/`q4_0`**, but the
   two field reports differ in sign because one box had the CPU pool on the critical path and the other had a
   99.7 % hit rate. Our `--cpu-moe` arms are the first condition and our pack's experts are **Q4_K (0.986)**, so
   the expected effect is small - and it is a **build flag**, i.e. two source builds and two starts, or an
   explicit "we do not test this". (Gated on a build decision.)
6. **`LLAMA_MOE_RANDOM_ROUTING=1` is the control our cache ladder never had** (section 7): random distinct
   expert ids after the router, graph and fused router unchanged. Merged **2026-11-18T11:34:41Z**, so it is in
   the **first tag after `b12503`** and nowhere yet. With it, a rung's hit rate can be measured against a routing
   distribution that does not depend on the prompt - which is what separates "the cache works" from "this text
   happened to be cacheable". (Gated download, then one ladder.)
7. **Gyro-S on this box is now a measured candidate, not a rejected pack** (sections 2, 8, 14). Someone with
   **our card, our OS and a 32 GB version of our RAM** reports **43 tok/s decode**, **1,300 tok/s prompt at
   30-60K**, **19.6 GiB of experts in RAM**, **4.40 GiB of VRAM cache at 64K**, **8/8 recall** and a
   **319-request soak with 0 failures**, on **release binaries**, and the same ternary types are **not**
   servable by any llama.cpp tag we can name. The cost is real - **58.5 GB** for `S-TQ1_0`, **29.7 + 28.8 GB**
   for `-noPLE` plus the IQ4_NL table - and the pack has **moved revision twice since 11-05**, so a row pins a
   revision. (Gated download.)

Items 1-4 are edits, 5 is a build decision, 6-7 are the gated download. **Nothing was started, no engine was
