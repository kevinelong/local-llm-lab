## 2. [GH] The 0.1.66 decode regression on **≤13 GiB cards** is **one auto-armed default**, bisected twice in this window, and it is **still present in 0.1.67**

This is the day's item, and it lands on our card's *gate*, not just its class: the auto-arm is
conditioned on **VRAM ≤ 13 GiB**.

**`#7154`** (2026-11-25T14:22:06Z, 1 comment, and the comment is the tracker bot asking for a title
prefix - so **no maintainer reply yet**). The mechanism, with source lines:

- **`l3_pf_auto_default`**, introduced in **`d28c053e6`** ("perf(low-vram): optimize 8 GB & 12 GB
  consumer GPUs", **v0.2.20 / engine 0.1.66**), auto-arms **static expert row slices + L3 prefetch**
  when **all** of: **VRAM ≤ 13 GiB**, **resident slots × 2 < total experts** (coverage < 50 %), and
  **pool workers ≥ 8**. Location: `engine/include/strata/core/low_vram_rules.hpp:134-140`.
- With static slices armed, `ExpertPool::drain()` takes the **`per_worker_`** path
  (`pool.cpp:1314`): each of n workers gets a static 1/n row slice and serially computes **every**
  expert in its slice, so **every phase finishes at the speed of the slowest worker**.
- On a **hybrid P/E CPU** roughly half the workers land on E-cores at ~half throughput, so each
  phase is gated by an E-core worker: **per-miss latency on the CPU pool roughly doubles**.
- **`STRATA_POOL_STATIC=0` does not disable it.** The condition is
  `want_static = want_pf || (st_env && atoi(st_env))`, so the auto-arm overrides an explicit 0.
  **Only `STRATA_L3_PF=0` clears `want_pf`.**

Measured on the reporter's rig (**i9-13900H 6P+8E, RTX 4080M 12 GB over OCuLink ~6.1 GB/s,
Qwen3.8-Flash-Next IQ3_S, 2,062 resident slots = 8 % coverage, `--pool-workers 9`**) - **same hour,
same model, 3×300-token hot average**:

| configuration | decode tok/s |
|---|---|
| 0.1.52 (control, same hour) | 50.5-51.7 |
| 0.1.66 best tuned config, static slices **on** | 18.4 |
| **+ `STRATA_L3_PF=0`** | **33.9 (+84 %)** |
| + `STRATA_POOL_DEP=0` | **39.7** |
| `POOL_DEP` back on (isolated cost) | 34.1 (**-13 %**) |

and the **CPU pool probe** (same machine, 10 threads) is the cleanest statement of the cost:
**0.1.52 123-133 us (16.7 GB/s)**, 0.1.62 146-155 us (13-14 GB/s), **0.1.66 static-on 204-587 us
(3.5-10 GB/s)**, 0.1.66 static-off 108-183 us (11-19 GB/s). **Verified still present in 0.1.67**,
`l3_pf_auto_default` unchanged.

**Independently bisected in the same window, on a 12 GiB sm_86 card, with a paired A/B** -
`rugabunda` in **`#6799`** (2026-11-25T12:39:53Z), crediting `@gtrak`'s bisect to the same

and on **`standard-v1`, fresh boot**, `STRATA_L3_PF=0` reads **short 76.3 / 4k 76.5 / 16k 82.0,
sanity 12/12** where stock 0.1.68 read **49.3 / 44.2 / 46.3**. Its user-observed power note (not

**The two other halves of the same default, both filed inside the window and both open with 0
maintainer comments:**

- **`#6948`** (2026-11-24T01:35:29Z): **`low_vram_pin_slots()`** also keys on **cache fraction, not
  card size** - any cache below 25 % resident pins 25 % of its slots. A 24 GiB card running a 72 GB

  **33.8 -> 35.1** (+3.8 %) with pinning off. The PR **turns pinning off above 16 GiB only**, which

- **A third 12 GB dataset in `#6799`** - `plsfullmarkprofessor` (2026-11-24T23:59:36Z), **RTX 4070 Ti
  12 GB sm_89, Windows WDDM**, UD-Q4_K_XL, DFlash2, `--kv int8 --kv-resident 32768`: **0.1.65 short

**Applies here?** **Directly, and the gate is satisfied three ways over**: 12 GB ≤ 13 GiB, our caches
are **6 % coverage** (1,500 slots of 24,576 in the 0.1.47 ladder), and `--pool-workers` defaults to
**9**. Our CPU is **hybrid (Ultra 9 285, 8 P + 16 E)**, so the E-core gating applies; the link is
**x16**, so the *frequency* of CPU-pool misses should be lower than on an OCuLink box - the effect is
expected **smaller, not zero**, and `POOL_DEP`'s `-13 %` is link-independent. Note the corollary this
gives our own record: the 2026-10-19 thread ladder (**8 threads 30.4 / 16 threads 32.3 / 24 threads
29.5 tok/s**) is the same shape as an E-core-gated pool, from the other engine.
**Test:** the `standard-v1`-shaped A/B this lab already runs - **stock vs `STRATA_L3_PF=0` vs
`STRATA_L3_PF=0 STRATA_CACHE_PIN_FRAC=0` vs `+ STRATA_POOL_DEP=0`**, interleaved, 2 rounds,
`standard-v1` sanity 12/12 as the readback, and the **CPU pool probe time** logged if the build prints
it. **Cost:** four starts on an approved engine, no download if 0.1.47 is used (but 0.1.47 predates
`d28c053e6`, so this is **a prediction about the bump, not a testable regression on our current
engine**) - and that is the honest limit of it: **our rows cannot be re-read as affected, and a bump
row cannot be trusted without this arm.**

So **the build gate moves to `>= b12720`** for mainline DSpark, and the 11-23/11-24 statements
("mainline llama.cpp has no DSpark support", the pack README's own line, and the `unslothai/llama.cpp`
fork requirement) are now **retired for the drafter**.

**What the thread contains that a row must carry:**

- **A real stall, found and fixed in review**: `--spec-draft-sampling probabilistic` with
  `--spec-type draft-dspark` **hangs on the first request** (no tokens after 60 s, core maxed, GPU
  idle) in `common_sampler_sample_and_accept_n_rejection`. `jcsq6` traced it: `draft_recurrent`
  builds **`q` as the full vocabulary (~200K)** and the residual loop calls `prob_of(q, ...)`, a
  **linear scan**, once per candidate - and the Inkling GGUF defaults to `top_k 0`, so one rejected
  draft costs **~4 × 10^10 comparisons**. Credit to the bot review for keeping it open
  ("the head commit still builds `q` densely over all ids"), and the fix is to detect the
  **id-indexed dense prefix of `q` and hash the sparse tail**: **O(V) per rejection** instead.
- **A second declared limit**: `src/llama-context.cpp:2362-2371` now **rejects non-block token
  ubatches** for the INKLING2 variant with a clear error, because `draft_batch` always sends full
  uniform blocks.
- **AI disclosure is on the PR**, and the target family is ours: `Inkling-2-Mini`, arch
  `inkling2_loop`.

**Also merged in-window and on our axes, one line each:**

- **`#32948` "inkling2 graph with fewer ops in mix() and control()"** (2026-11-25T03:54:32Z, first in
  **`b12723`** - ahead 0, behind 0). **This one is text-neutral by construction and by test**:
  `llama-perplexity --kl-divergence` against master gives **mean KLD 0, same top-p 100 %** on CUDA
  (40×512), Metal (4) and CPU (2); greedy decode of 96 tokens is the same text on CUDA and Metal.
  Ops: concat **548 -> 413**, rms_norm **514 -> 400**, repeat **120 -> 0**, fill **51 -> 4**; GB10
  tg128 **40.44 -> 41.37** (+2.3 %), pp512 **832 -> 857** (+3.0 %). So unlike the model commits of
  11-24, **this one does not move a text-identity boundary**.
- **`#32965` "cuda : fix int overflow of the MMVF matrix offsets"** (2026-11-24T19:26:10Z). `mul_mat_vec_f`
  multiplied block indices by strides as `int * int`, so an offset past **2^31 values wraps** and the
  kernel reads out of bounds - **an illegal memory access**, not a wrong number. It bites
