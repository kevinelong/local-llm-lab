### 3.1 [GH][VRAM] #3725 - the auto pinned complement ignores the WDDM shared segment, and the error names the wrong thing

**Source.** `altiber`, filed **2026-10-22T13:30:50Z**, **0 comments**, **patch inside**. Windows 11,
**Strata v0.2.2 (`011c9e60`)**, engine **0.1.47 built from source** (CUDA 13.0, archs `86;120`), **Q2_0**
native pack, layer split over **RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB + RTX 3070 8 GB**, Ryzen 7 9700X, **31 GB RAM**,
`--resident-experts --max-context 131072 --kv int8`, MTP `--spec 4`.

- **What happens, with the engine's own lines:** `room for the complement: 14.81 GiB (19.06 GiB available
  commit minus 4 GiB headroom and a 0.25 GiB margin)` -> `mapped pinned cache complement ready: resident
  14.81 GiB, pinned 14.81 GiB in 6 s; page-locked and mapped` -> `prefill: the token id buffer (out of
  memory)` -> `EngineDied`. **Twice, at 14.81 and 14.91 GiB. Starts that pinned 10.3-10.6 GiB work.**
- **The failing allocation is ~26 KB** (`cudaMalloc(chunk * 4)` in `Prefill::init`), so the message points
  at a token buffer while the cause is a ceiling.
- **The cause, and it is the useful half:** *"Under WDDM every page-locked mapped host buffer counts
  against the GPU's shared (non-local) segment, on every CUDA device it is mapped into, and Windows caps
  that segment at about half the RAM (here ~15.5 GB)."* The Windows counter
  **`GPU Adapter Memory\Shared Usage`** reads **11.98 GB on both adapters with a 10.5 GiB complement**, i.e.
  ~1.5 GB of other mapped buffers on top. **Commit capacity does not show this limit at all.**
- **The fix, as measured by its author:** ask DXGI (`IDXGIAdapter3::QueryVideoMemoryInfo(0,
  DXGI_MEMORY_SEGMENT_GROUP_NON_LOCAL)`, `Budget - CurrentUsage`) on each NVIDIA adapter, take the
  smallest, keep **2 GiB** for buffers mapped after the complement (`STRATA_WDDM_SHARED_RESERVE_MIB`
  overrides), clamp. Result on the same PC with full auto and no manual budget:
  `WDDM shared segment: 12.97 GiB left on the tightest NVIDIA GPU, 2.00 GiB kept` ->
  `the complement is limited by the WDDM shared segment: 12.90 -> 10.97 GiB` -> `pinned 10.97 GiB in 5 s`
  -> **READY in 25 s**, serving since. The patch is a diff in the issue against
  `src/core/expert_source.cpp`; **unmerged and unmentioned in any release note.**

**Applies: yes, as a ceiling and as a diagnostic.** Yesterday's §2.1 stated the rule from
`FreeToken` #55 (**13.77-13.82 GB** of `cudaHostRegister` on a 32 GB box). This is the same rule measured
from the other side, on a **31 GB** box, with **~15.5 GB** as the segment - so the half-of-RAM rule now has
two independent measurements on two engines at two sizes. On **this** box (63.46 GiB) it predicts a
**~31.7 GB** ceiling `[INFERENCE]`, which our 12 GiB arm is nowhere near and our once-tried 24 GiB arm is
still below - **but the 24 GiB arm tripped on physical RAM, not on this**, so nothing recorded is
invalidated. What *is* new: the ceiling is **per CUDA device**, it counts **only mapped** buffers, and its
failure surfaces as an unrelated allocation error.
**Instrument:** the `GPU Adapter Memory\Shared Usage` perf counter read beside `nvidia-smi` during one
resident arm - no download. **Cost:** one engine start plus a counter read.
