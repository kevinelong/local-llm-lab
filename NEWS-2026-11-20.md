### The four defaults that reach this box, read from the release's own text

- **`STRATA_ROUTE_TAIL_SKIP` is now Off by default**, and this reverses the field this lab added on 11-16 (*"declare
  STRATA_ROUTE_TAIL_SKIP=0 in every Strata row"*). Vendor's words: *"people reported quality loss, so it is off
  unless you turn it on"*; **Control sets `STRATA_ROUTE_TAIL_SKIP=0` for every start unless you set the variable,
  and setup writes it into every Unsloth config, so engine 0.1.62 and older also run with it off under this
  version. Engine 0.1.63 itself reads an unset value as off on every backend."* The price of Off on one card is
  named: 2 x RTX A4000 IQ3_XXS **50.8 -> 47.0 tok/s** two cards and **39.2 -> 35.5** one card; on an RTX 5090 the
  mean decode is **94.5 On against 73.4 Off (x1.29)**. **Consequence for us: the field becomes a readback, not a
  declaration** - a row now reports what the log says, and the default is the safe one.
- **`--short-read` changed from 64 to 16 tokens on compute capability 12.0**, and **our card is a named
  exception to its own identity gate.** Of the ten test boxes, the RTX 5070 line reads: *"10 of 10 with
  `--short-read 64`, **8 of 10 at the defaults**"*, against Mac M5 Max 10/10, Arc A750 9/9, RTX PRO 5000 9 of 9
  with 64 and 7 of 9 at 16. **So 0.2.17 changes text on our card at its defaults, by construction, and the switch
  that restores identity is `--short-read 64` or `STRATA_SM120_PERF=0`.** No text-identity claim crosses
  0.1.60 -> 0.1.63 on a 12 GB cc 12.0 card unless it pins that flag.
- **`STRATA_ADAPT_FETCH=2` is on by default for cc 12.x single cards of 16 GB or less** - our card's size class,
  reported on an RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB: decode **1.02 to 1.23 of** the off figure by request kind (4,096-token prompt
  **35.5 -> 40.7 tok/s**, tool call **51.1 -> 62.6**), with **9 of 48 prompt rounds giving the same tokens** and
  forced-token KL 0.948. **The qualifier matters more than the gain:** *"It runs in the arena mode (the model fits
  in RAM); in the resident RAM mode (`--resident-budget-gib`, 28 in our test) the engine does not use it and says
  so at start-up."* **Our chunk-ladder and resident-ladder arms all declare `--resident-budget-gib`**, so this
  default probably does not reach them - which is a start-line read, not an assumption.
- **Setup keeps `RAM - 17` GiB instead of `RAM - 24`** on a PC where the model does not fit in RAM. The vendor's
  own table on a 64 GiB box, UD-Q4_K_XL, RTX PRO 4500: budget 40 gives 97.7 GB of SSD read for a 65K prompt and

  but **a later 8 GiB program was killed**. **Not our pack:** the rule is stated for `UD-Q4_K_XL` and `UD-IQ4_XS`

- **`STRATA_WDDM_SHARED_RESERVE_MIB` (default 2048 MiB)** is new for **two or more** CUDA cards (#3725) - one card

## 2. [GH] `#6406`: the DFlash2 wide draft window is worth **+89 % decode**, and its own limits clause names our card as excluded

Created **2026-11-20T14:17:01Z**, open, 1 comment. This is a PR-shaped proposal against
`engine/include/strata/core/draft_cap.hpp` and `terminal/strata_control/drafting.py`.

- **What it does:** lowers the desktop-Blackwell threshold for the wide DFlash2 window from **48 GB to 15 GB**
  (`kDflash2BlackwellW8Bytes = 15'000'000'000ull`), so `--draft dflash2` runs `--spec 8` instead of the clamped
  `--spec 6` on cc 12.0 cards of 15 GB or more, for **+11.3 MiB** of device buffer (68.5 vs 57.2 MiB total).
- **Measured on an RTX 5080 16 GB, Windows 11 WDDM, driver 621.14**, Swift Qwen3.8-Flash-Next GSQ-RCO IQ2_XS with
  `dflash2-tuned-selq4.pack`: **review 8K 230.4 vs 122.3 tok/s (+89 %)**, review JS 5K **161.3-163.6 vs 148.0**,
  counting **238.7 vs 232.4**, draft acceptance **81.6 % (307 of 376)**, greedy output identity verified 100 %.
- **Why it is not ours:** *"Cards under 15 GB (such as **RTX 5070 12 GB**) and DGX Spark (`sm_121`) intentionally
  remain capped at 6 to avoid tight VRAM overhead on lower-tier cards."* Our card is named twice in the same
  paragraph - once as the excluded example and once in the code comment being replaced
  (*"A consumer RTX 50 card with less memory was not measured and keeps the older values"*).
- **The reachable question is therefore not "is +89 % real" but "can the cap be raised by hand"**: the proposal notes
  *"`--draft-cap` is not exposed in the GUI"*, which implies a CLI flag exists. If it does, our card is one start
  away from the wide window - and the constraint that binds is the **11.3 MiB** buffer plus whatever the 12 GB card
  already cannot spare (our own records stop the cache ladder at **5,376 MiB with 683 MiB free**).

## 4. [GH] `#6402`: `/v1/models` publishes the context window and **no output cap**, so a client guesses 32,000 where the server enforces 32,768

Created **2026-11-20T13:03:11Z**, open, 0 comments. The context half of #5945 is fixed in 0.2.17 (#6116):

  config it reports **32,000 against a real 32,768** - and *"for any config whose `max_tokens` is lower than
