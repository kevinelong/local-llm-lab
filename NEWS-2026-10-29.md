## 2. Strata - the reserve is a contract, and the chunk/trim mechanism got its first 5070 row

### 2.1 [GH][VRAM] `--vram-reserve-mib` is enforced once, at startup, and never again

**#4516**, created **2026-10-28T16:41:26Z**, 3 comments, open. Environment: **RTX 3080 Ti 12 GB** (compute-only, display
on a second card), Windows 11, WDDM, **engine 0.1.52** from the v0.2.7 release tarball, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next IQ3_S,
single GPU, `--max-context 262144 --kv int8 --kv-resident 32768 --spec 5 --spec-min-p 0.6 --mtp --vram-elastic`.
Startup cache: **2414 slots / 4.64 GiB**.

The A/B is one variable and one request body (17,155-token Chinese prompt, `max_tokens=50`, non-streaming, captured
through an API tap):

| `--vram-reserve-mib` | expert cache | free VRAM during the request | prefill tok/s |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| **0** | - | ~0-496 MiB | **843** |
| **1024** | 1912-2015 slots | ~780 MiB | **1,289 (+53 %)** |

The sweep, in the reporter's own order:

| reserve | outcome |
|---:|---|
| 100 | **engine death** |
| 500 | landing 254 MiB (*"add --vram-reserve-mib 758"*), elastic cache grew **2180 -> 2437 slots**, free VRAM hit **0 MiB mid-session** |
| 758 | the floor the engine itself prescribes |
| **1024** | landing 778 MiB, **best prefill** |
| 2048 | cache **~500 slots smaller, no gain** |

Mechanism, from the report: *"the engine sizes the expert cache once at startup against `--vram-reserve-mib` and never
re-checks whether the actual free VRAM stays above that contract. During a long session the actual free VRAM drifts
below the contract (driver reservations + `--vram-elastic` growth filling freed VRAM), the WDDM page manager starts
paging engine allocations (including expert-cache pages) to system RAM, and prefill throughput drops 53 % on identical
requests"* - and *"the engine never detects or recovers: the paging is invisible to CUDA applications"*. Two fixes are
proposed and neither exists yet: a free-VRAM watchdog that shrinks the cache by ~512 MiB and logs it, or `cudaMemPool`
eviction-priority hinting.

Two consequences for us, and both are corrections of emphasis rather than of number:

- **Our 2026-10-27 finding was right for a reason we did not have.** We recorded *"900 beats 2000 by +0.5-4.4 %
  prefill because it buys 3000 slots against 2168 - its decode half is withdrawn"*. This report's 1024-vs-2048 row is
  the same result on another 12 GB card with the mechanism attached: **above the paging floor, more reserve only costs
  slots.** Our 900 sat above the floor (758 on their card) and 2000 gave nothing back.
- **A row that goes below the floor measures paging, not cache size.** Our ladder's lowest reserves were 900 and
  1536 - both above 758 - so no recorded arm is invalidated. But the gate has no rule for "did free VRAM fall below
  the declared reserve mid-request", which is exactly our gate rule 3 shape one level down, and `--vram-elastic` is a
  flag we have **never** recorded (it is in this reporter's config and appears in no note of ours).

**Applies:** yes, directly - 12 GB card, WDDM, Windows, our engine family. **Instrument:** the chunk/reserve runner,
adding a free-VRAM floor check and `--vram-elastic` to the declaration list. **Cost:** an edit, no engine. A run of the
sweep itself would be four starts and is gated on the bump.

### 2.2 [GH][VRAM] The first RTX 5070 row: pinned arena, stage trim, a fixed K, and the clamp gone

**#4603**, created **2026-10-29T12:11:18Z**, closed the same day by the maintainer (*"results like these will be added
there when it launches"*). Measured **2026-10-29** on **0.1.52 self-built with `CMAKE_CUDA_ARCHITECTURES=120`**, a
**RTX 5070 12 GB (48 SMs) + RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB (36 SMs)** pair, both sm_120, IQ3_S of Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, 262,144
tokens, native mode, `--kv int8 --spec 4 --spec-min-p 0.5 --mtp`.

Result: decode **73.3 -> 92.9 tok/s**, prefill **2,481 -> 2,722.7**, from two opt-in settings tested one at a time:

| configuration | arena pinned | slots | decode tok/s | prefill 54k tok/s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| default under WDDM + `layer_split: auto` | 7 GiB | 5,151 | 67.7-73.3 | 2,449-2,481 |
| `+ STRATA_ARENA_PIN_GIB=40` | **39 GiB** | 6,147 | 86.8 | 2,835.8 |
| `+ STRATA_STAGE_TRIM=1` **with a fixed K** | 39 GiB | 4,361 | **91.0-92.9** | 2,664.7-2,722.7 |

Four mechanism statements in it are worth more than the numbers:

1. **The engine caps its own host registration at 8 GiB on a split under WDDM**, and says so:
   `cudaHostRegister limited to 8 GiB by the engine (multi-GPU under WDDM, or remote experts); 8 slices pinned (7 GiB)`.
   `STRATA_ARENA_PIN_GIB=40` overrides it; `=0` ("pin everything") stops at the same 39 GiB because the **driver**
   ceiling is `RAM/2 - 8 GiB` (~54 GiB on a 125.5 GiB box). `[INFERENCE]` on this 63.46 GiB machine that driver ceiling
   would be **~23.7 GiB**, against the ~17.8 GiB of `Shared Usage` we measured on 2026-10-22 - so our single-card
   llama.cpp measurement sits *under* the ceiling and the ceiling was never what bit us.
2. **`STRATA_STAGE_TRIM=1` is a no-op on one card, and prints why.** Verbatim from the report's section 8:
   `STRATA_STAGE_TRIM=1 prints --trim-stage-weights needs a layer split across GPUs (ignored) on one card`, and
   *"`STRATA_ARENA_PIN_GIB` only matters for a multi-GPU run; a single card registers the whole arena regardless."*
   **This closes our 2026-10-27 item** - we wrote *"`STRATA_STAGE_TRIM=1` still changed nothing on one card"* and
   inferred *"the #4261 refund is a split effect"*; the engine says the switch was **ignored**, which is a stronger and
   checkable statement, and the same line should be grepped for in our own logs.
3. **On a split, `layer_split: auto` silently disables trim on the first card**, because that card builds its arena
   before the split search runs (`generate.cpp:3845`). Trim alone: slots **3,732 -> 4,361 (+16.9 %)**, decode
   **83.0 -> 91.0 (+9.6 %)**.
4. **The slot-count-to-chunk clamp, on our card, in our context size:**
   `WARNING: prompt chunk 7168 tokens, not 8192: CUDA1 (RTX 5070) has 1770 expert-cache slots, and an 8192-token chunk
   borrows 1692 of them`. After trim + K=27 the card lends **2,747** slots and the line becomes
   `prompt chunk auto: 8192 tokens` - which is where the prefill gain comes from. **Third independent witness of the
   #4261 mechanism, and the first on a 5070.**

Two further results that matter to rows we hold:

- **Decode and prefill optima do not coincide.** The split sweep at K = 24/25/26/27/28/30 gives decode **86.8 / 83.8 /
  91.5 / 93.6 / 93.1 / 86.4** and prefill **2,835.8 / 2,873.8 / 2,750.4 / 2,697.4 / 2,615.4 / -**: decode peaks at
  K=27 (**+7.8 %** over `auto`), prompt at K=25 (**+1.3 %**), and K=27 costs 4.9 % of prompt speed against K=24.
- **Code prompts decode 13-17 % faster than prose with identical flags, because the drafter lands more often** - drafts
  accepted **61.8 %** on Russian prose (92.9 tok/s) against **83.7 %** on a Laravel/Vue refactor prompt
  (**104.9-108.6 tok/s**), and the speculator sweep says the default `--spec 4 --spec-min-p 0.5` is already optimal
  (3/0.5 -> 104.9, 5/0.5 -> 101.8, 4/0.7 -> 104.4, 4/0.3 -> 104.1).

One card each, same context, same prompt, at 32,768 (where 262,144 does not fit one card): **both 97.8 tok/s**, 5060 Ti
alone **88.6**, 5070 alone **84.6** - and the split is *inverted*, because the 5070 wins prefill (**2,433 vs 1,811**,
48 SMs vs 36) while the 5060 Ti wins decode by holding **79 % more cache slots (3,685 vs 2,054)**:
*"decode with MTP is bound by cache hits, not by compute."*

**Applies:** the single-card statements (2) and (3) yes - they change what our own record means. The split numbers no.
**Instrument:** the chunk ladder, with the trim line grepped. **Cost:** an edit now; starts after the bump.

### 2.3 [GH][VRAM] Windows commit headroom exhausted by the DeepSeek auto RAM store - our binding gate, in the wild

**#4605**, created **2026-10-29T12:37:54Z**, 2 comments. Windows 11 (10.0.26200), i7-12700, **128 GB** DDR4, RTX 5060
Ti 16 GB, driver 610.88, official `0.1.52-windows-x86_64-cuda-cuda13` (BUILD `a42cc3d7df70`, exe SHA-256
`e2f9fce5...e610`), DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 ISTA **IQ2_XS / 2.25 bpw**, 8K context, six experts, DSpark auto.

The automatic RAM-store sizing chose **101.3 GB** and the machine ran out of *commit*, not of RAM:

| field | bytes |
|---|---:|
| SystemCommitCharge | **145,636,839,424** |
| SystemCommitLimit | 145,729,966,080 |
| free commit at the event | **93,126,656** (93 MB) |
| `strata-dsv41.exe` CommitCharge | 118,554,161,152 |

*"Windows logged event 2004 and the host began failing allocations"*, and a helper reported
`Failed to load advapi32.dll: The paging file is too small for this operation to complete`. The pagefile was **initial
4096 MiB / maximum 8192 MiB**, system-managed = false, and a later run's commit limit read 141.435 GB against this
event's 145.730 GB - the reporter explicitly does *not* assume the pagefile stayed fixed.

The two workaround arms, both declared as not-single-variable:

| store | outcome |
|---|---|
| 101.3 GB (automatic) | resource exhaustion before any functional request; tree terminated for recovery |
| 80 GB (`--ssd-cache 80 --reserve-mib 1536`) | functional checks and a 1K/4K pair, then **guard-stopped at 94 % commit** (132.953 of 141.435 GB) |
| **64 GB** | **36 requests over 1,844 s completed**; sampled commit peak **118.529 GB**; decode medians **7.1 / 7.2 tok/s**, first-content **9.7 / 19.3 s** |

The finding: *"the store sized 101.3 GB"* was computed from **physical** RAM free after the GPU's weights

arithmetic sees the commit limit**. Our 2026-10-22 measurement is the same wall from the other side - **25.7 -> 0.84 GiB

The first comment is a second witness with a named lever: **edt-xx, 2026-10-29T13:01Z** - *"Loading the PLE table into
ram was the problem and `--ple-io direct` fixed it, so the whole OS was not trying to run in ~3G of ram, paging and
causing Signal 15 timeouts ... Though there was suposed to be code leaving a ~10G buffer for the os? Is it being ignored
here?"* So `--ple-io` has a third documented mode and it is the one that keeps the host out of the paging regime.
**Our 0.2.7 notes list `--ple-io ram` (+16.5 % at 1K, +30.6 % at 4K) as a *speed* setting; nobody has priced its
commit cost.**

**Applies:** yes as the general shape, and `--ple-io direct` directly (our packs are PLE models). **Instrument:** the
memory gate, which already reads CIM commit, plus a `--ple-io` declaration. **Cost:** an edit; a load test is gated on a

### 2.4 [GH][ENGINE] Two channels of non-determinism, both with a code path

- **An adaptive kernel crossover that is itself noisy changes the greedy answer between starts. #4547** (created
  **2026-10-28T21:35:51Z**, 0 comments), Intel Arc Pro B70, engine 0.1.51 vs 0.1.45, `tools/ab_engine.py`, greedy,
  256 new tokens, fresh engine per start. At a 6,345-token prompt, `STRATA_PREFILL_MMQ` unset (= `auto`) gives
  **1,107-1,118 tok/s** and greedy answer lengths **134 / 145 / 134 / 129** across four starts; `off` gives
  **1,316** and **151 every time**; `=1` gives **1,376 / 1,366** and **143 / 143**. The engine's own line explains why:
  ```
  int8 experts, measured here (...): us per expert at 16/32/64/128/256 rows,
  int8 17.5/30.5/36.0/49.8/83.4, FP16 29.7/31.8/36.4/47.5/67.2 -> crossover 72 rows
  ```
  and over 11 starts the crossover read **32, 40, 40, 40, 41, 72, 149, 151** rows *"and 139, 151, 154 on three starts
  whose device string ends `|22|20|640x2560`"*. The FP16 timings are steady (36.4-36.6 us at 64 rows); **the int8 timing
  at 64 rows swings between 31 and 45 us**. The cache that should make this a one-time measurement,
  `~/.cache/strata/i8-crossover.txt`, is written **inside the `--rm` container** whose home is not a mounted volume -
  so every start re-measures. **This is a second mechanism for "temperature 0 is not seed-stable"**, distinct from
  #1928's drafter-EMA path, and it is *arithmetic*, not timing of the model.
- **A three-state switch whose documented `0` never reaches the engine. #4546** (created 2026-10-28T21:35:49Z,
  0 comments): `sycl/serve/strata-sycl.sh` forwards `STRATA_*` variables but **skips every one whose value is `0` or
  empty**, which is correct for an on/off switch and wrong for a three-state one where unset means `auto`. So
  `STRATA_PREFILL_MMQ=0` (the documented FP16 path) starts the `auto` path instead, and `"off"` works only because
  `choose_mode()` parses a non-digit string as 0 - which no document says. The report lists eight more `=0` forms the
  Intel docs use that the launcher cannot deliver: `STRATA_I8_FORCE`, `STRATA_I8_WG`, `STRATA_MMVQ_A2`,
  `STRATA_MMVQ_WIDE_32`, `STRATA_LEND_MIRROR`, `STRATA_ARENA_ALIAS_CHECK`, `STRATA_SPLIT_HEADROOM`, `STRATA_MIRROR_MIB`.
  **The general lesson is ours to take**: a declared env value is not a delivered one, and the check is the engine's
  own log line, not the config file.

**Applies:** (1) as a mechanism, (2) as a discipline - our declaration blocks are env-var boards. **Instrument:** the
determinism control (three greedy passes in one start, plus one after a restart) and the log-line readback we already
require for flags. **Cost:** two starts, no download.
