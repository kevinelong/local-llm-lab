### 2.6 [GH][VRAM] The chunk is capped by prompt-path buffers, and the fix is bitwise

Two items, both open, both on our axis:

**#4039** (created 2026-10-25T01:07Z, updated **2026-10-27T21:47Z**, 2 comments), `STRATA_PF_GROUP_SCRATCH` and
`STRATA_PF_HC_REGION`, both opt-in and off by default. The mechanism, verbatim: *"A streamed prompt chunk sends every
non-resident expert over PCIe once, so on a card where the experts don't fit, the chunk size sets how often an expert

slots it borrows."* And: *"the MMQ path's gate/up output, its SwiGLU output and its q8_1 copy are sized for the whole
chunk (T\*K rows, ~84 KB a token), although a group's products read them and are done before the next group starts."*

## 4. Ollama - a memory estimate 3-4x too high, and the PLE is why

### 4.1 [GH][VRAM] `gemma4:e4b`'s predicted VRAM is 3-4x the real number because CPU-mapped PLE counts as GPU

**#19357**, created **2026-10-28T09:53Z**, 0 comments. `llama-server`'s own allocation lines for the model
(Q4_K_M, `num_ctx` 16384, 43/43 layers on GPU) show `CPU_Mapped model buffer size = 525.00 MiB` and **`5376.00 MiB`**,
`CUDA0 model buffer size = 2829.67 MiB`, KV 272.00 + 42.50 MiB, compute 221.30 MiB; `ollama ps` reports **3.12 GiB, all
in VRAM**, with *"about 5.9 GiB of the weights stay[ing] in CPU-mapped memory."*

| `OLLAMA_NUM_PARALLEL` | `num_ctx` | `num_batch` | predicted | actual VRAM |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 16384 | 1024 | 8.1 GiB | 3.12 GiB |
| 1 | 16384 | 512 | 10.6 GiB | 3.12 GiB |
| 2 | 32768 | 512 | 12.2 GiB | ~3.4 GiB |

For contrast, `gemma4:12b` (no per-layer embeddings) at 32k *"is predicted at 9.0 GiB against 7.55 GiB actual. That is a
normal margin."* The effect: on a 16 GB card the two models *do* fit together at 11-12 GB, but the scheduler logs
`predicted to exceed available memory, evicting` and evicts whenever the order is unfavourable.

Diagnosis offered: *"It looks like the CPU-mapped per-layer embedding weights of the Gemma 4 E-series are counted as GPU
memory."* **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next is a PLE model** and this is the first report that names the accounting error, not just
the symptom.

**Applies:** as the general shape - a scheduler that charges host-mapped weights to VRAM over-predicts and evicts. Our
