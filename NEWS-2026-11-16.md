## 3. [GH] `STRATA_ROUTE_TAIL_SKIP` is on by default on CUDA and changes answers

**`#5136`, still open, 3 comments:** `STRATA_ROUTE_TAIL_SKIP` is **enabled at rank 7 by default on CUDA builds and
on HIP gfx1200 / gfx1201**. An expert that is not resident and that every token of a verify window routes at rank 7
or lower is **neither copied nor computed** - its row is zeroed and the survivors keep the router's weights without
renormalisation (`src/core/expert_source.cpp:4941-4944, 5163-5166`). The consequence the reporter draws is the one
that matters to this lab: *"the skip keys on live cache state... the same prompt with the same seed can therefore
differ at two points in one server's lifetime, and differs between machines with different VRAM or cache size."*
The engine already disables it under `--batch` for exactly that reason. `setup.py:4107-4108` writes
`STRATA_ROUTE_TAIL_SKIP=0` into the run config **only** for Unsloth packs outside the RAM-budget mode and writes
nothing for any other family; `tools/quant_quality.py:40` pins it off in its own runs.

**Applies to us directly.** Our Strata rows were taken on engine **0.1.47**, which predates the switch, so nothing
recorded is invalidated - but a bump to 0.1.60 inherits a default that moves output and depends on residency.
`#6091`'s config, on the same engine, sets it to 0.

## 4. [GH] Two Windows memory items, both about a number that is read once and then trusted

- **`#6089` (open PR, base `c77572806` = main): cap the WDDM expert-cache shrink step at 256 MiB.** Under WDDM the
  engine queries `device_free_bytes()` right after the expert cache allocation, and the driver's reporting lags -
  occasionally returning under 16 MiB. `generate.cpp:9022` then computes `give` from `want` (which already
  includes `--vram-reserve-mib` plus `pipe_first`) and **drops 1.0-1.4 GiB in one iteration**; if the second read is
  still low it chops again. Measured on **RTX 5080 16 GB (sm_120) + RTX 4060 Ti, Windows 11**, `--layer-split auto`:
  vanilla loses **~1.4 GiB twice (-2.8 GiB)** and settles at **3,823 MiB free against a 1,076 MiB reserve, 2,747 MiB
  of dead space**, with **6,215 slots**; the bounded version lands at **1,061 MiB free, 7,976 slots (+1,761)** and
  GPU1 goes 7,746 -> 7,949. In a layer split the same over-reduction also starves the cost model and **drops K from
  22 to 17**. `#6091` reports the panic never fires on its own 4090 rig (54 boots), so the defect is
  configuration-dependent, not universal.
- **`#6082`: the PLE residency policy and the `#2090` chunk guard collide on Windows.** On a **96 GB Windows PC**
  the policy locks the **26.8 GiB n-gram table** because table + arena + headroom appear to fit, and what is left
  then trips the guard: **prompt chunk 21,248 -> 8,192**, prompts **9-14 % slower**, start 22 s longer, the
  conversation cache drops to 2.8 GiB or off. Direct reads on the same SSD cost nothing measurable (the startup
  probe says 118,000-146,000 rows/s). The mode is decided at **84.9 GiB available (table locked) against 84.0 GiB
  free (direct)** - i.e. **a browser open in another program flips it**. Not our capacity, but the guard-chunk
  interaction is ours, and our chunk is already a declared field.
