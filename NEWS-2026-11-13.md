## 3. [GH] Ollama - the YaRN shift fix exists, is 32 lines, and is blocked

**`#19680` "llama.cpp: keep context shift amplitude-neutral"**, created **2026-11-12T18:50:32Z**, **+31 / -1 across 2
files**, `Fixes #19457`, state **`open`**, `mergeable_state` **`blocked`**. The mechanism, in its own words: with
`--context-shift` every shift re-ropes keys that are **already** roped, and `build_rope_shift` passed the forward-pass
RoPE params through unchanged - including the **YaRN amplitude factor** - so each shift multiplied every cached key a
second time, and the reused `--keep` prefix was rescaled the same way. Affected models named: **deepseek-r1, phi3/phi3.5,
phi4-mini, olmo-3/olmo-3.1**. The fix sets

```cpp
rp.attn_factor = rp.ext_factor != 0.0f && rp.freq_scale > 0.0f
                 ? 1.0f / (1.0f + 0.1f * logf(1.0f / rp.freq_scale))
                 : 1.0f;
```

so the shift scale is exactly **1.000000** and only the rotational delta is applied.

### 4.1 `#17732` (and its fix `#17733`) - below the RAM threshold, every prompt reloads the model from disk

Created **2026-11-13T13:50:28Z**, reproduced with `--disable-all-custom-nodes` and three core nodes. When free RAM is
below the **`--cache-ram`** threshold, `PromptExecutor.execute` calls, **after each node** (`execution.py` ~808-820 on
master `dc9ffe6add`):

1. `ram_release(ram_headroom, free_active=True, min_entry_size=RAM_CACHE_LARGE_INTERMEDIATE)`
2. on Linux `should_free_pins_for_ram_pressure()` is always true for a shortfall, and with no pins to free
3. `ram_release(ram_headroom, free_active=True)` evicts **everything, including the current generation's outputs**.

In `RAMPressureCache.ram_release` (`comfy_execution/caching.py` ~561) **only entries whose outputs are all dynamic are
skipped** for the current generation, so a `CLIP`/`ModelPatcher` output of a **non-dynamic** model (e.g. `--highvram`) is
evicted - and its `ram_usage` estimate **counts only CPU tensors**, so evicting it frees next to nothing while forcing a
full reload. `#17733` (*"Keep current model outputs in the RAM pressure cache"*) is the open fix; `#17729` caps the
eviction exponent to avoid a float overflow.

**Applies:** directly, as a **new undeclared covariate for the image track**. Our arms admit on **free VRAM (0.5 GiB
floor)** and score **same-prompt-same-seed PNG bytes**; this defect is a function of **free RAM** and of whether the model
was loaded through Dynamic VRAM. Two image runs that both clear the VRAM floor can therefore differ only because the
box's free RAM crossed a threshold. **Cost:** one log read for the `--cache-ram` value and any eviction line, then one
same-seed pair (~15 min). **Gated** on the card being idle.

### 4.2 `#17723` - Adaptive Projected Guidance changes the result at cfg 1.0, which the node's own guard intended to prevent

### 8.1 `2611.15671` - "The Noise Floor of a Quantization Comparison: Engine, restart, batch and kernel drift, measured against 8-bit and 4-bit effects in two decision models"

2,400 public decisions, two of Cloudflare's decision models (**Clef-flash 9B**, **Clef 27B**), one **NVIDIA GB10**. It
states every quantisation effect as **excess over the non-quantisation sources**, and it measures them:

| source | decisions changed |
|---|---|
| same stack, restart | **bit-identical on every record, both models** |
| vLLM restarts, BF16 / FP8 / NVFP4 | **0 / 5 / 20** (Clef-flash), **4 / 0 / 22** (Clef) |
| engine change at BF16 | **6-16** |
| batch-size change | **8-15** |
| **Gated DeltaNet kernel transformers selects unasked** | **10-14** |
| quant **inside one engine**, Q8_0 | **0.88 % / 1.04 %** |
| FP8 | **2.12 % / 1.62 %** |
| Q4_K_M | **5.79 % / 3.50 %** |
| NVFP4 | **5.79 % / 5.67 %** |

Ceiling on all non-quantisation sources: **0-0.92 % of decisions**. **"Every 4-bit interval excludes every
non-quantisation source."** Two of **five pre-registered predictions failed**, and the same two fail again on the 27B:
**FP8 is not inside restart noise**, and **changing engine moves fewer decisions than 8-bit quantisation does**. The
accuracy result is the one that changes a reading: over 31,275 public questions, Clef's **Q4_K_M moves accuracy by a net
six questions (-0.02 points, [-0.20, +0.16])** while **436 of its decisions get worse**, and NVFP4 costs Clef-flash
**0.95** and Clef **0.65** points. Code, quantisation recipes, per-record outputs and the pre-registration are released.

**Applies:** directly, to two of our own instruments. (a) Our repeat-noise floor is **sub-1 % on a 160-token probe**
(2026-10-16 q3), and this gives that figure an independent measurement on a *decision* instrument - **0.92 % is the
ceiling**, so a decision-model arm that moves fewer than ~1 % of records is not measuring the arm. (b) Our decision rows
report **accuracy**, which this shows can be flat while **436 records flip** - the metric to add is the **share of
records whose label changed**, beside accuracy, with the interval. **Cost:** a spec edit now; a re-score of retained
artifacts is a **normal session** (the evidence is under `ai_bench`), not this scan.

### 8.2 `2611.15842` - "Fewer Expert Transfers Without Changing Routing: Expert Eviction for MoE Offloading"

OLMoE-1B-7B on an L40S, in a harness that performs and times **real CPU-to-GPU copies**. Its finding inverts the premise
of a whole family: *"every tested policy that holds an expert set fixed and decodes faster than the least-recently-used
(LRU) cache increases next-token loss by at least **0.067 nats**."* The change that leaves every routing decision intact
does better - when the cache must evict, **evict the expert whose next use lies farthest in the future** - which copies
**2.1x fewer experts than LRU at no loss in quality** and is within **3.5 %** of the fewest copies possible. It needs
future demand: **the next sixteen tokens of demand recover 96 %** of the reduction, whereas **a rule based on counts of
past requests recovers 27 %**.

**Applies:** directly, and it prices our own routing prior. Our **`STRATA_ROUTE_PRIOR=1.5`**: **+22.9 % teacher-forced
perplexity** for +24 % decode (2026-10-28), and **AIME -10.0 points for +45 %** (2026-11-01). This paper says that class
of lever - *changing which experts are resident so routing changes* - costs at least **0.067 nats** in the cleanest
measurement anyone has published, which is the direction our perplexity row already pointed. **Instrument:** our
teacher-forced perplexity plus a task-level arm. **Cost:** an engine with routing we can change; not scheduled.
