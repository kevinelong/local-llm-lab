## 4. [GH] `#6249` is the first thing in five days that actually addresses the append re-read: keep the short last chunk instead of a second streamed pass

PR **`#6249`**, **2026-11-19T03:50:07Z**, 0 comments, opt-in. `NEWS-2026-11-17` established the defect (`#6136`:
0.1.60 re-reads a whole chunk when 9 tokens are appended, because `equal_cut_rule.hpp` moves every boundary), and
`NEWS-2026-11-18` struck the two candidate fixes (`--prompt-cache-tail` live-and-inert; `STRATA_PLE_AUTO_RAM=0`
buys chunk 8192 -> 32768 and loses the page-locked tier).

**The mechanism, in the author's words:** *"engine 0.1.60 reads a prompt segment that is longer than the chunk in
equal chunks, also when the old cut's last chunk would be short. Each equal chunk is a full streamed pass. With a
native pack and the CPU share, **a read a few tokens over the chunk costs about 2.9 s more than a read that fits**."*
The switch is **`STRATA_PREFILL_EQUAL_TAIL=N`**: "a segment whose old last chunk is under N tokens and under the
stream floor keeps one full chunk plus that short last chunk". Unset, nothing changes.

**Measurement:** RTX 4080 SUPER 16 GB (**RTX 4060 Ti 8 GB as `--expert-cache-device1`**, PCIe 4.0 x16 / x4),
Ubuntu, CUDA 13.4, UD-Q4_K_XL, control 0.1.60, both engines from the v0.2.14 source **`09b6038`** with the release
cuda13 flags (`-DSTRATA_MMQ=OFF`, sm_89 only); serve path, two requests on one conversation, three rounds, fresh
engine per round, **arm order alternating and target order rotating**, median of 3; auto chunk **16,128** in every
arm. `tail` sets `N = 3,072`, the CPU share's floor, so every short last chunk is kept.

This is the **fourth** attempt at the append cost and the first with a mechanism attached rather than a knob. It is
a PR, not a release, so it is in no engine we can download today.

**Applies:** yes - our arms are agent turns, i.e. appends. **Instrument:** the append metric the 11-17 file made
first-class (`append_cost_ms`, `append_reread_tokens`), run at our 65,536 with a 9-token append. **Cost:** a build,
so gated.
