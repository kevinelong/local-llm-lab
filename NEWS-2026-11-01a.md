### 1.1 [GH][ENGINE] The prompt-path knob that cannot fire under `--parallel` (Strata #4929)

- **`STRATA_PREFILL_HELPER_SOURCE` is gated on `o.batch <= 0`.** The reporter's framing: *"it never engages with
  `--parallel N`; it halves prompt TTFT when it does"*. Open, unmerged report, **one** box (four cards), **no** maintainer
  reply at 15:07Z. Treat the "halves" figure as the reporter's own before/after, not a vendor row.
- **What the two log lines establish, independent of the knob.** With `--parallel` in use the prompt path *lends* cache
  slots to the prefill and then finds **none of the borrowed experts in RAM** (`0 of 1277`), so it re-reads them from the
  SSD **on every chunk**, and the planner prices the whole prompt at **271.2 GB of drive reads per 32,768 tokens**. That
  is a prefill-side twin of the reserve tax we measured in `CHUNK-LADDER-AND-RESERVE-2026-10-26.md`: a budget lent to one
  path stops being available to the path that was ranked for it.
- **Applies to this box? Partly, and not the way it looks.** Our **12 GB VRAM / 32 GB RAM** cannot hold a 63.2 GiB expert
  set at all, so "all experts in RAM" is not an option we are being denied - the SSD path is our normal case. What
  transfers is the **gating shape**: a knob whose effect depends on a flag combination we would have to check by reading
  the log, exactly as with `STAGE_TRIM` (2026-10-29) and the inert `STRATA_MMVQ_IL` (2026-10-21).
- **Instrument and cost.** One serve-mode arm: same prompt at `--parallel 1` and `--parallel 2`, `STRATA_PREFILL_HELPER_SOURCE`
  on/off, four interleaved pairs, reading the `lends ... cache slots` and `0 of N fit in RAM` lines back. Our q1 ladder
  already uses the TTFT line; cost is **one session, no download**, provided we still have the 0.1.55 binaries.

### 1.4 [GH][ENGINE] NVFP4's high scale byte: undefined on Blackwell, and it cannot come from llama.cpp's own quantizer (llama.cpp #31079)

- **The defect:** non-OCP Vulkan NVFP4 path, `ue4m3_fp32_lut` is **128 entries**, indexed by the raw scale byte;
  `ggml_validate_row_data` accepts every byte, the CPU decoder masks bit 7 but compares the full byte against 0 and 0x7F,
  so **a byte >= 0x80 indexes past shared memory**. Patch sizes the table, both init loops and two host shared-memory
  budgets to **256**, and costs **512 more bytes of shared memory per workgroup** in the affected shaders.
- **The survey that makes it useful, author's words (2026-11-01T10:22-10:54Z):** *"llama.cpp's conversion and
  quantization do not produce it from the start"*; CPU, SYCL and WebGPU **ignore** the high bit; CUDA FP8, Vulkan OCP
  and HIP CDNA3 treat it as a **sign bit**; **"Blackwell native leaves it undefined"**; *"I only tested"* (survey partial).
- **Applies to this box?** Not today - we are CUDA and we load Q4_K/IQ-class files, and we do not have a Vulkan path we
  use. It matters as a **third-party-file rule**: a non-llama.cpp NVFP4 stream on sm_120 lands in the "undefined"
  branch, so an NVFP4 arm from an unfamiliar publisher would need a row-data validation pass first. Cost: zero until such
  an arm is proposed.

### 1.5 [GH][ENGINE] `STRATA_SPEC_PCUM`: a drafting-window rule that measures neutral-to-negative (Strata #4856)

- **PR**, opened 2026-10-31T21:05:33Z, **updated 2026-11-01T15:05:04Z**, open, **0 comments**, `Keoian`. The rule: end an
  MTP window where the **product** of the drafted tokens' top-1 probabilities falls below a depth-scheduled threshold,
  because "four drafts at p = 0.85 pass a 0.8 floor; all four are accepted about 52 % of the time" (`[INFERENCE]`
  0.85^4 = 0.522, consistent). Unset, *"byte-identical to main on the P100 machine"*.
- **Its own measurement:** 2x RX 5700 XT (HIP), Q2_0 - greedy text **byte-identical** with the rule on, decode **+1.5 %**
  when sampling, unchanged greedy. 2x Tesla P100 (CUDA), IQ3_XXS - greedy unchanged on short prompts, sampled **within
  noise (-4 %)**, and **4-6 % slower at 33 K and 110 K** of context. Greedy text **changes** when the rule is on.
- **Applies to this box?** No, and the report says why in its own words: *"the gain is small or negative, because this
  model's verify cost hardly grows with depth"*. Our 12 GB box is verify-cost-dominated in the other direction (long
  context, short outputs), so this is a **decline with a reason**, not an untested item.
